Research: Connecting Materials and Manufacturing

Hybrid Manufacturing-Development of wire-arc metal additive and subtractive manufacturing equipment, optical-fiber-based structural health monitoring, and a novel optical-fiber integration method using material-extrusion 3D printing. Related research covers materials ranging from metals to composites, including CFRP laminate fabrication and metal 3D printing. The durability of composite materials and their structural health monitoring are also investigated to enhance hydrogen-storage safety.
