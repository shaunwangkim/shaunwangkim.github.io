Additive Manufacturing & Optical Fibre Integration

A novel optical fibre integration method by embedding sensor during 3D printing, realizing in-situ residual strain measurement of additive manufacturing for the first time and structural health monitoring of the part in later application.

Context

The new embedding technology is proposed and verified. The special equipment has be made and the composite tank having optical fiber is produced in Lab. The work examines residual strain, strain transfer and temperature coupling in fibre optic sensing for structural health monitoring.

Researcher

Outcomes

  • Published studies on in-situ residual strain, time-dependent strain sensing and strain–temperature decoupling. The technology has been applied in Type-V and Type-IV high pressure hydrogen storage vessels worked in automotives.
